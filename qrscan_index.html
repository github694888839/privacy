Privacy Policy

App: Qrono – Scan & Generate
Developer: MobileLumen
Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Qrono (“the App”) handles information when you use the App on Android or iOS. We designed the App to work primarily offline and to collect as little personal data as possible.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or phone number to use core features.

  • QR / barcode content you scan or generate — processed on your device and stored locally in scan/generate history (unless you delete it).
  • App preferences — such as theme, language, and premium (ad-free) status, stored locally on your device.
  • Device permissions — Camera (to scan codes) and Photos / Gallery (to scan from images and save generated QR codes). Permission access is used only for these features.

We do not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, or microphone data.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide scanning, generation, history, and sharing features
  • Remember your settings on this device
  • Show ads (for free users) or honor ad-free purchases
  • Improve stability and fix crashes when diagnostic data is provided by the OS / store

4. Local Storage

History and settings are stored locally on your device using on-device storage (for example SharedPreferences). Clearing app data or uninstalling the App will remove this information.

5. Third-Party Services

Depending on your platform and whether you purchased ad removal, the App may use:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — to show banner ads to free users. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data according to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase — to process optional ad-removal purchases. Payment details are handled by Google or Apple, not by us.

If you use “Open link” on a scanned URL, your device browser will open that third-party website under that site’s own privacy policy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by the third-party services listed above solely to operate ads or purchases. We do not operate our own cloud backend for storing your QR history.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Local history remains on your device until you delete individual items, clear history in the App, clear app storage, or uninstall the App.

9. Your Choices

  • Deny camera or photo permissions (some features will be unavailable)
  • Delete history or uninstall the App to remove local data
  • Purchase ad removal (if offered) to stop AdMob ad serving in the App
  • Use OS / Google / Apple ad personalization settings where available

10. International Users

The App may be used worldwide. Processing by advertising or store providers may occur in other countries subject to their policies and applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
